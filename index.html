How to Play Solve the Mystery
The Goal
Find the culprit — the only person who was alone with the Laptop in the Demo Room. Click "Accuse" once everyone is correctly placed.
Place all 4 figures so that no two share the same row or column (Sudoku logic). Blocked cells (furniture) cannot be used.
The grid has 4 rooms of 2×2 cells each. The room someone ends up in is key — use it to identify the culprit!
Controls
Click a clue card to turn on note mode, then click empty cells to mark them with that person's initial. Click the card again to turn it off.
Drag a portrait from the bench (bottom left) onto a free cell — or click it, then click the target cell.
Click a placed figure to pick it up, then click another cell to move it there.
Drag a figure on the grid to a new cell to move it, or back to the bench to remove it.
Conflicts
Cells with a red border have a conflict — two figures in the same row or column. Fix them before you accuse!
